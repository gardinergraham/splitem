Privacy Policy

Last updated: 6 June 2026

Split-em is a children’s creativity app for making funny mix-and-match characters. This policy explains what information the app uses.

Information We Collect

Split-em does not require an account and does not collect names, email addresses, location data or personal contact information.

Photos

If you tap the camera button, Split-em asks permission to save the character picture to the device photo library. The image is saved on the device. Split-em does not upload the image to a server.

Speech

Split-em uses the device text-to-speech system to speak generated character names. The app does not record the child’s voice.

Analytics, Ads And Tracking

Split-em does not use third-party advertising, behavioral tracking or analytics in this version.

Children’s Privacy

Split-em is intended for family-friendly creative play. The app is designed to work without collecting personal information from children.

Changes

This privacy policy may be updated if app features change. The latest version will be published on this page.

Contact

For privacy questions, please contact the developer through the App Store listing or the support contact details provided with the app.
